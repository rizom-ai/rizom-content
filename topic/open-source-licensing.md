---
title: Open Source Licensing
---
Rizom’s intellectual-property framework combines open-source licensing with clear trademark governance. The core runtime uses AGPL-3.0 to discourage proprietary hosted forks and closed commercial capture, while the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces use Apache licensing to enable partners to build and commercialize extensions. A Developer Certificate of Origin is planned to clarify contributor rights and support clean intellectual-property succession. Rizom B.V. will initially hold copyrights and trademarks, with a planned transfer to an independent Rizom Foundation.

Trademark rules distinguish the open software from controlled use of the Rizom name, logo, product names, wordmark, and certification marks. Descriptive references and required attribution are permitted, while permission-based uses, domains, package names, namespaces, and Certified marks are subject to published policies, registry controls, or partner approval. Forks and modified versions must use distinct names and avoid misleading association. Enforcement should begin through dialogue, with formal escalation for misleading commercial use; the policy remains subject to legal review and trademark filing. This structure protects the software commons and brand integrity while enabling professional services, implementation, and broad commercial participation.
