# INC Partnership — Three-Horizon Plan

Internal strategy. Drafted 2026-08-06, after Geert Lovink's reply committing INC's interest. Not for external sharing: the plan depends on the story being told in INC's voice, not ours.

## The big picture

The institutions that housed critical thought are shedding it — INC is the leading edge of a Europe-wide pattern, not an anomaly. Every defunded institute and orphaned research community will face the same question INC faces on 1 September 2026: where does intellectual life live when the institution is gone? Rizom's thesis, taken seriously, is the answer: intellectual life that organises itself out of what people already hold, with no centre and no institution around it.

INC is the first instance of the pattern and the most symbolically perfect one: the institute that spent twenty-two years theorising networks becomes one. If INC 2.0's long-term vision (which Geert says does not yet exist) becomes "the first post-institutional research community running on infrastructure its members own," INC gets a future that continues its own argument, and Rizom inherits the lineage: thirty years of Amsterdam net critique — DDS, Waag, nettime, INC — gets an infrastructural successor. That genealogy is the category, and only its owners can confer it.

The measure of success, at three years: a living network of self-owned brains among the people who invented network critique, and a category others ask for by name — with Rizom as its substrate.

## Horizon 1 — the founding act (now to autumn 2026)

What must be true: the vision is co-authored, not sold.

- The call with Geert (this week, while he is free in France; back at HvA from 17.8) is an editorial meeting, not partnership logistics. Agenda: "here is what I think INC 2.0 is."
- Deliverable: a short joint text, in the INC tradition of making concepts by naming them in public, articulating the post-institutional condition and the network of brains as its answer. If INC 2.0's long-term vision and this text are the same document, the partnership is constitutionally embedded rather than contractual.
- INC's own brain goes live as node zero, after v0.2.0 stabilises. Capped unfunded commitment: one brain, operated by Rizom, biased toward publicly visible surfaces (archive, publishing).
- When the stichting is founded and the exclusive licence-back is executed: invite Geert onto the stichting board as independent member — the lineage institutionalised in governance.

Falsifier: if the joint text does not happen, the vision was not shared.

## Horizon 2 — the first community (through 2027)

What must be true: the milieu uses it, visibly.

- A cohort of 10–25 people from INC's world, each holding their own brain; first federation slice so they can find and cite each other.
- The cultural artifact this horizon exists to produce: INC publishes and convenes about the network in their own voice — a reader, an event streamed through VOID, the network as research object. This is the moment the category gets named by people with standing to name it.
- Product work this pulls (already on the roadmap, sequenced by the arc, not forked): collective-posture validation, onboarding friction as funded/first-class work, ATProto discovery, identity and provenance.

Falsifier: if the cohort does not actually use it, the thesis fails at the friction layer.

## Horizon 3 — the category (through 2029)

What must be true: INC stops being the only instance.

- Two or three more post-institutional communities running — INC's network is where every candidate is visible from.
- The genealogy written by others; the category asked for by name; Rizom as substrate.

Falsifier: if no second community appears, this was a partnership, not a category — still worth having, but a different thing.

## Operating inversions

- The story is always told in their voice, never Rizom's. Marketing budget for this: zero, by design.
- Product priority follows the arc — federation and friction rise because the vision needs them.
- The services business and funding windows are fuel, never steering. Grant applications (Eureka/GlobalStars Sept 2026 if its two gates clear — RVO eligibility of the vereniging, Taiwan consortium consent — then SIDN, Creative Europe, Stimuleringsfonds in their recurring windows) slot into horizons; they do not define them. September 1 only cliffs signature-dependent grant work; the relationship itself survives INC's dissolution.
- Watch the falsifiers honestly; each horizon has one.
