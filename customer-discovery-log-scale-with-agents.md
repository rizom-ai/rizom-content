# Customer Discovery Log: Scale with Agents

*Companion to [[beachhead-scale-with-agents]]. Running log — add an entry after every discovery call or pilot conversation for this beachhead. Keep entries short: who, what they said, decision, next step. This is the evidence base for [[assumptions-register-scale-with-agents]] and [[value-proposition-tests-scale-with-agents]] — update those when an entry here moves the needle.*

## Log format (copy for each new entry)

### [Date] — [Company / contact name or anonymized descriptor]
- **Source:** how the conversation came about (outreach, warm intro, inbound)
- **Fit signals:** what matched or didn't match the ICP in [[beachhead-scale-with-agents]]
- **What they said:** direct quotes or close paraphrase on failure modes, current agent setup, pain
- **Qualify / disqualify:** decision and why
- **Assumptions touched:** which numbered assumptions from [[assumptions-register-scale-with-agents]] this call provides evidence for or against
- **Next step:** pilot proposed, follow-up scheduled, disqualified, etc.
