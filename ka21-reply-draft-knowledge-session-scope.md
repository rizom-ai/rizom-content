**Subject: Re: Knowledge Session — scope of the first phase**

Dear Helmut, dear Wolfgang,

Thank you. I agree with your framing: the €5,000 phase should do three things—give KA21 a repeatable way to assess opportunities in education, determine whether this school is the right first pilot case, and produce the playbook that any implementation would be built against.

I suggest we structure it in three steps:

1. **Prepare with KA21.** We define what makes a use case valuable and feasible, which questions to ask, what a Rizom Brain can and cannot do, and what would be required from a customer in terms of people, knowledge, data, ownership and budget.
2. **Assess the school as a live case.** We begin with a short exploratory conversation with the director. No wider commitment is needed at that point. If there is a good fit, we run short one-to-one interviews with the relevant staff followed by a half-day workshop. The method asks each person what they know that nobody else does, and what breaks if they are not there tomorrow. That is what surfaces the real problems, lets us test possible use cases and identify the strongest one.
3. **Make a clear recommendation.** At the end, you receive a playbook: the map of where knowledge actually sits, which doubles as the specification any implementation is built against. With it, a go/no-go assessment for the school, a proposed use case and its prerequisites if the answer is yes, and a practical qualification guide that KA21 can reuse with future customers. We would also outline the scope and commercial basis of an optional implementation.

Initially, the school only needs to be willing to have an open conversation about its staff-side processes and problems. If we proceed further, it would need to provide access to the relevant people and materials. We do not need pupil data for this phase, and at this stage the school is not committing to a pilot or purchase.

The €5,000 phase stands entirely on its own. Afterwards, KA21 remains free to decide whether to commission the additional €10,000. Those five months end with two things in your hands. First, a working application: a Brain with an agent, built for the use case we identify and tested with a real user. Second, the ability to run it without us—the software is open source and carries no licence fee, and handover includes deployment documentation, an operations guide and training, so that your team can stand up an instance for the next school yourselves. What we build for this use case—the knowledge model, the agent's behaviours, the templates—is the toolkit you reuse.

The €10,000 holds for a pilot of the shape we have discussed—six months, one application, co-delivered—and phase one is where we confirm it against the use case we actually find.

If this school is not the right case, there is no further obligation; we can use what we learned to assess another opportunity in education. And if you would rather run further sessions before committing, that is sensible—the point of phase one is to give you something solid to decide with.

Further Knowledge Sessions can also be offered jointly to potential customers as paid discovery engagements, and those need not come out of your own budget. Each is €5,000 to the customer, split 60% to whoever delivers, 15% to whoever brought them, and 25% to the commons that funds the open platform. On sessions we deliver together, the delivery share is split by role and agreed before we start; once you run them yourselves, you keep 75%.

One session will not validate the whole private-school sector, but it should give us a tested starting point, a concrete customer assessment and a method KA21 can apply elsewhere. Co-delivering the full six-month pilot is itself the partner qualification: it is earned on real work delivered together, not by examination.

I hope that gives you the common understanding you were asking for. If any part of it does not match how you see it, tell me which and I will adjust it—better settled now than during the session.

If it does match, I will send a short written scope for the €5,000 phase, including how and when we approach the director, so that nothing happens there before you are ready. We can then agree a start date.

Best regards,
Jan Hein
