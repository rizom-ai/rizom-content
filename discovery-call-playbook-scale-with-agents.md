# Discovery Call Playbook: Scale with Agents

*Companion to [[beachhead-scale-with-agents]]. Use on first calls with prospects matching the Scale with Agents ICP. Goal: qualify fast, disqualify honestly, and get to a paid pilot decision — not a generic discovery chat.*

## Opening framing (say this early)

"We help B2B SaaS teams around 10 to 40 people get their first AI agent from 'sometimes works' to reliably production-grade — by building the context layer the agent's actually missing, not by changing the model. We run a paid two-week pilot: pick one workflow, we build a context layer around it, and measure the lift against your current setup head-to-head."

## Qualifying questions

1. What agent workflow have you tried, and where does it break down or get corrected most often?
2. Is the underlying process itself well-defined and agreed on internally?
3. Who currently owns fixing this — engineering, ops, or nobody yet?
4. What would "reliable enough to trust" look like, in numbers (completion rate, time saved, escalation accuracy)?
5. Would you be open to a paid two-week pilot with a clear before/after comparison?

## Signals to listen for (good fit)
- They can point to a specific workflow and specific failure modes, not just "AI isn't working great"
- They already suspect the model isn't the bottleneck
- There's a named owner for the fix, with budget authority or a clear path to it
- They can articulate what "good" would look like in numbers

## Disqualifiers (end the call warmly, don't force it)
- No live or recent agent experiment — too early, nothing concrete to diagnose yet
- The workflow itself is undefined or contested internally — a context layer won't fix a process nobody agrees on. This is where a knowledge layer risks uncovering an operating-model problem rather than a documentation problem.
- They want general-purpose "AI search," not a production workflow fix

## After the call
Log the conversation in [[customer-discovery-log-scale-with-agents]]: who, what they said (direct quotes on failure modes are gold), qualify/disqualify decision, next step. Update [[assumptions-register-scale-with-agents]] if anything on this call moves an assumption's status.
