---
title: Outcome-Based Cooperation
---
Rizom distinguishes between inputs, outputs, and outcomes when evaluating cooperative work. Inputs are raw resources such as time, money, and materials, while outputs are the deliverables produced from those resources, including documents, software, or meeting records. Outcomes are the forms of value created by those deliverables, with Rizom emphasizing mission-aligned value rather than money alone. In the current model, ambassadors who share the organization’s mission and vision represent an important outcome. This framework shifts attention from activity and production toward the lasting value generated for the organization and its community. It provides a durable basis for assessing collaboration and community growth.
