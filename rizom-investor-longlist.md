# Rizom — Investor Longlist

Research checked 7 September 2026. Working discovery list based on [[rizom-investor-target-profile]]. **28 named investment organisations: 11 priority research, 13 conditional, 4 hold. None contacted; none has confirmed willingness to invest in the foundation/operator structure or accept the commons obligations.** Research-priority bands are not investment probabilities or confirmations of interest. People are public professional research entry points, not confirmed deal owners, personal angel prospects, or established warm introductions.

Current proposal: EUR 500k total, EUR 400k initial close plus EUR 100k milestone tranche. Foreign-currency tickets must be checked against both total and initial-close size; no exchange-rate conversion is assumed. Founder is willing to consider relocation, dilution, further equity, loss of majority control and operator sale, while protecting independent foundation-owned core IP and durable commons funding. The 25% current allocation is reviewable with evidence and independent foundation agreement, not unilaterally by investors. Relocation/restructuring are options, not commitments. This ordering supersedes the earlier local investor-shortlist.md. Local detailed data: investor-longlist.json, investor-longlist.csv and investor-longlist.md in the fundraising directory.
