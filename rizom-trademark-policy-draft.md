# Rizom Trademark Policy — Draft

Companion to [[certified-partner-model-decisions]] and [[certified-partner-model-mutual-expectations]]. Drafted 2026-07-29. Status: internal draft for legal review; publishes at rizom.ai once the wordmark filing is submitted. Not yet legal text.

## Why this policy exists

Rizom's code is a commons: the runtime is AGPL-3.0, the SDK and contracts are Apache-2.0, and nothing in this policy limits any right those licenses grant. The name is different. Trademark law requires a mark's holder to control how it is used, or the mark dissolves — and with it the ability of clients to know that "Rizom Certified" means what it claims. Free code, guarded name: this policy is the guard.

## The marks

- **"Rizom"** (wordmark) and the Rizom logo
- **"Rizom Brains"** (the product name)
- **"Rizom Certified"** and derived designations ("Rizom Certified Partner", "Rizom Certified Practitioner")
- **"Rizom Certification candidate"**

**Holder:** Rizom B.V. (interim). The marks will be assigned to the independent Rizom Foundation (stichting) upon its establishment, which will then steward this policy. This interim arrangement is deliberate and temporary.

## Uses that need no permission

Truthful, descriptive statements are always free:

- "Built on Rizom Brains", "powered by Rizom Brains", "compatible with Rizom Brains"
- "We deploy and operate Rizom Brains" — services on the open-source software, branded or priced however you choose
- Referring to Rizom, linking to rizom.ai, writing or teaching about the platform or methodology
- Distributing **unmodified** official builds under the product name, with attribution
- Community advocacy: talks, posts, meetups about Rizom, provided no affiliation or endorsement is implied

## Uses reserved to the registry

- **"Rizom Certified"** and its derivations may be used only by partners currently listed in the certification registry, and only in the standing the registry shows. The registry at rizom.ai is the single source of truth; a listing lapsing ends the right to the mark with it.
- **"Rizom Certification candidate"** may be used only by candidates listed as such in the registry, during their certifying engagement. Candidates never use the "Rizom Certified" mark itself.
- Nobody certifies others in Rizom's name. Certification is issued only by the marks' holder.

## Uses that require permission

- Using "Rizom" in a company name, product name, service name, domain name, or social-media handle in a way that implies affiliation or endorsement
- Using the logo other than as an unmodified reference to Rizom
- Merchandise bearing the marks

Ask: trademarks@rizom.ai. The default answer to good-faith community requests is yes.

## Forks and modified versions

The licenses guarantee your right to fork and modify the software. The name does not travel with the code:

- A modified version or fork ships under **your own name**. You may state, truthfully, that it is "a fork of Rizom Brains" or "based on Rizom Brains".
- Do not use "Rizom" in the fork's name, logo, domain, or package names.
- The `ai.rizom.brain.*` lexicon namespace is governed separately by the contracts repository policy: extensions live in a namespace you control and must not redefine `ai.rizom.brain.*`.

## Rules of use

When using the marks as permitted: use them as written — no abbreviations, translations, or modifications; don't combine them into your own logos or marks; don't suggest sponsorship, partnership, or endorsement beyond what the registry states; and on first prominent use in commercial material, attribute: "Rizom is a trademark of Rizom B.V." (after assignment: "of the Rizom Foundation").

## Enforcement

We start every trademark conversation as a conversation. Most issues are misunderstandings and end with a small wording change; we escalate only when someone knowingly trades on the marks to mislead clients — because the people harmed by that are the certified partners and clients who rely on the marks meaning something. Disputes involving certified partners follow the process in the partner agreement: written reasons, a 30-day window to respond or remedy, appeal to the board.

## Changes

This policy may be revised by its holder; the current version at rizom.ai governs. Changes never retroactively convert good-faith use into infringement.
