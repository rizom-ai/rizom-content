---
title: Mission-Aligned Financing Strategy
---
Rizom’s financing strategy combines earned services revenue, non-dilutive funding, and selectively deployed growth capital to build a self-sustaining open-source commons while protecting core intellectual property and maintaining capital discipline. Paid Knowledge Sessions, pilots, and recurring retainers provide the primary commercial engine, with grants, subsidies, R&D support, and technology credits funding open-platform infrastructure without equity dilution. External funding—including a proposed EUR 250,000–300,000 raise, UNIIQ, bridge financing, or revenue-based financing—should serve as growth capital rather than survival capital, accelerating hiring, partner certification, reference deployments, cohort operations, and commercial capacity. Conservative, base, and growth scenarios should test whether funding can advance the trajectory by approximately 18 months, with hiring and deployment gated by recurring commons revenue, cash-positive operating performance, and milestone-based thresholds. Key sensitivities include retainer churn, pilot delays, and the uncertain ability of certified partners to generate recurring commons revenue. Separating ecosystem infrastructure funding from commercial operations supports transparent, mission-aligned capital allocation without dependence on high-growth venture financing.
