---
title: Services Productization
---
The content describes the effort to turn bespoke organizational consulting into a repeatable service offering. A knowledge session is structured as a defined sequence involving diagnosis, facilitated learning, and a practical organizational playbook. The emphasis is on creating consistent methods, templates, and outcomes instead of relying only on an already-designed workshop. This approach makes a consulting offer easier for buyers to understand and easier for the provider to deliver consistently. The note also highlights the strategic tension between near-term services revenue and longer-term product development. A deliberate capacity limit is therefore treated as necessary to prevent consulting work from consuming scalable product work.
