# Rizom — Round Budget & Ask

*Bottom-up derivation of the round: the cost of running Engine A while Engine B gets built, over 18 months. Companion to [[rizom-how-this-scales]] (the scaling story) and successor to the two-round plan in [[fundraising-strategy-and-decisions]]. Settled 2026-08-31.*

## The shape

One round, **€500k** on a SAFE/convertible. No separate friends & family ask — early money is simply first money into this round. Two tranches:

- **Tranche 1 — €400k at close.** Runs the machine for 18 months.
- **Tranche 2 — €100k**, gated on the first team composed across brains earning its commons. Releases into the second engineer, productizing certification, and scaling the hosted fleet. The gate makes the milestone discipline visible from the outside: growth money is only spent on a mechanism that has demonstrated itself.

## What the money does

Every line is one of two things: **node inflow** (founder + closer + network seeding keep Engine A closing, and every engagement deploys brains) or **the composition layer** (the engineer building the TMS layer that turns nodes into a network). Nothing in tranche 1 funds scaling — that is tranche 2's job, after the gate.

## Sensitivities

- If Engine A produces zero revenue, tranche 1 runs out around month 14. The closer thesis must show signal by month 9. This is deliberate: the budget converts the known top risk (closing cadence) into a measurable checkpoint instead of a vague worry.
- Softest lines: the engineer's rate (±€1k/mo = ±€18k) and the closer guarantee (±€1k/mo = ±€12k). Noise at this round size.
- The founder line is benchmarked to an international raise (~€96k/yr against a $100–150k international seed band), not the NL-only market. Under-pricing it was rejected explicitly: an underpaid founder is a diligence red flag and re-creates the consulting income hedge this round exists to retire.
