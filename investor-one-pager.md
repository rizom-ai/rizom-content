# Rizom — Investor One-Pager (draft)

*For warm leads. Honest, foundation-led framing (Rizom B).*

## Rizom Brains
**Open-source living memory that organisations actually own.**

Organisations lose their working memory every day — the context behind decisions, the reasoning of a project, the expertise that leaves with people. The tools that promise to fix it are closed silos that turn your knowledge into someone else's asset. **Rizom Brains** is the opposite: an open, self-hostable "brain" an organisation fully owns — data as portable markdown on their own infrastructure, built on the AT Protocol, licensed AGPL so it can never be captured into a proprietary product.

### What we sell
Not software licences — **a practice**. We deploy and run brains for organisations, on a proven engagement (Survey → Workshop → Playbook → pilot → retainer), and we're building a **certified-partner network** so independent practitioners can deliver it too. The open platform is the commons; the practice, the brand, and the network are the business.

### Traction
- **Three live pilots** across distinct verticals — an **AI ecosystem**, **cultural archives/institutions**, and **regenerative-farming cooperatives** — each with a tailored proposition already in hand.
- Anchor engagement priced and scoped: **EUR 3–5k/month retainer + EUR 10–20k build**.
- Platform live, self-hostable, and open source under AGPL.

### Business model — 60 / 25 / 15
Every engagement splits **60% delivery · 15% acquisition · 25% commons** (the commons share funds the platform + the foundation that stewards it). Current prices are **early-adopter/traction discounts** — standard pricing is ~2x (EUR 35k builds, EUR 7k retainers). Real, untapped pricing power.

### The structure — and what your equity buys
The platform IP and the "Rizom" mark move to an **independent foundation** (AGPL commons) — deliberate, and the reason clients trust there's no lock-in. **Rizom B.V.** — the entity you'd invest in — owns the **commercial practice, the certified-partner registry, client relationships and services revenue, and a perpetual licence-back to the platform.** You're backing the company that operates on top of the commons, not a proprietary-software bet. This is honest by design; the openness *is* the moat.

### The ask
A **~EUR 45k bridge** to cover ~6 months while we convert the three warm pilots into recurring retainers and bring on a dedicated closer. **Not a growth round — a bridge to services self-sustainability.** Structure to suit (convertible / revenue-based / SAFE); sized to the milestone, not to a valuation we can't yet justify.

**Use of funds:** founder runway through the ramp · first commercial hire (acquisition) · pilot delivery costs.

**What sustainability looks like:** at these prices, **one retainer plus roughly one small engagement a month covers the business.** The path is short and the unit economics are already positive.

NLnet/NGI Zero (open-source grant, EUR 50k, applying), WBSO (R&D wage subsidy), and cloud/AI credits (AWS Activate, ~USD 5k+) — all fund the *open platform* without touching your equity, so your capital goes furthest.
