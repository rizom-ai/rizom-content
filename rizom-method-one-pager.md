# The Rizom Method

### Team memory you can map, repair, and own — built on thirty years of transactive memory research

**The invisible system.** Every organisation runs on a system nobody manages: who knows what, whose judgment is trusted on which questions, how information actually moves. It never appears on an org chart, but it decides whether work flows or stalls. And it is fragile — a key departure, a reorg, rapid hiring, or an AI rollout can break it overnight, and nobody can say what broke.

**The science.** Researchers call this a *transactive memory system* (TMS), one of the most consistently replicated findings in organisational psychology since 1985. Teams with a strong shared map of expertise measurably outperform teams of equal individual talent; peer-reviewed studies put high performers above 70% on TMS measures, while most teams start well below. The gap is structural, not personal — which means it can be diagnosed and fixed.

**What Rizom does with it.** Researchers measure transactive memory. Consultants write reports about it. Rizom installs it.

- **Diagnose** — structured interviews and a team survey locate the fragile knowledge: what does this person know that nobody else does, and what breaks if they leave tomorrow?
- **Map** — a facilitated session where the team builds its own picture: an expertise map, trust boundaries, an operating playbook. Everyone sees the same system, often for the first time.
- **Install** — the map becomes living infrastructure: a Rizom Brain the organisation fully owns — open source, self-hostable, no vendor lock-in — that keeps the memory current as people and projects change.

Each step is a priced engagement tier, from a fixed-price Knowledge Session through pilot to retainer. The method is the revenue ladder.

**Why it compounds.** The method is Rizom's transferable asset: certified partners deliver it under the Rizom name and remit a share to the commons — the practice scales without payroll. It is also the wedge into budgets that exist today: AI doesn't break an organisation's knowledge system, it exposes that there never was one — so the work is bought on AI budgets while fixing an organisational problem. And it is the long-term composition logic: when brains hold how people work, not just what they know, teams can be assembled on evidence instead of resumes.

**Where it stands.** Three pilots in progress across sectors — an AI ecosystem, cultural archives, farming cooperatives. The platform is live and open source. The first partner certifications run on real, paid engagements.
