# We sell the work, not the software.

*Rizom deploys and runs living memory — a Brain — for organisations. The software is open source; what you buy is a memory that works.*

**The product.** A Brain is shared memory for a hybrid human–AI team, built as working infrastructure rather than documentation. It captures from where work already happens — the chat where a decision lands, the email thread, the conversation — so it stays current without anyone maintaining a knowledge base. Humans and AI systems work from the same living memory, and a Living Memory Score tracks how well it is actually working: continuous and behavioral, derived from the work itself. The platform is open source (AGPL) and self-hostable — memory this important belongs to the organisation that creates it, so there is no lock-in to buy your way out of later.

**What you can buy.** Five products. Each stands on its own. Each one's result is the next one's starting point, if you choose to go on.

- **Team Workshop · €1,500 half-day in-house (up to 15 people), €750 online (90 minutes).** For a team that has just added AI and wants to work out how a team with an AI member actually runs. Together, the team draws who does what, who decides what, and what the AI is for. You leave with a shared picture. If the picture shows gaps, the audit is the next step.
- **Knowledge Audit · €5,000 fixed, three weeks.** An independent finding on where your organisation depends on individuals. Interviews with 6–10 people (what does this person know that nobody else does, and what breaks when they leave?), a half-day mapping workshop, and an audit report: where knowledge lives, whose judgment counts, where it leaks, and three to five interventions ranked by cost and impact. The report is yours; there is no next step you are obliged to take. Credits in full toward a build.
- **Build · €15,000 early-adopter (standard €35,000).** Two forms. A working Brain, deployed on your infrastructure or hosted by us, populated with one team's real memory and in daily use. Or a context layer for an AI agent you already run: the audit's map turned into the memory the agent is missing. Early-adopter pricing is earned, not timed — launch clients co-develop and stand as named references.
- **Retainer · €4,000/month early-adopter (standard €7,000).** Hosting, upgrades, and support — we run the Brain so your team just uses it. Monthly, cancellable: the exported system can always be self-hosted, so you stay because it works, not because you are stuck.
- **Partner track.** For organisations that want to open a whole field rather than fix one team: co-develop the first Brain application for their vertical and become the certified partner who delivers it there (see [[innovation-lead-pilot-offer-outline]]).

**Who this is for.** Organisations whose knowledge *is* the work — especially fields where knowledge leaves faster than it accumulates: young professional fields, high fluctuation, fast-moving practice. Current pilots run in innovation networks, sustainability, and art & academia.

**The foundation underneath.** The approach is built on transactive memory systems — four decades of organisational psychology on why some teams consistently outperform equally talented ones, extended by Rizom to teams that now include AI. The science, and why it matters now, is one more page: see the companion pager "Living Memory."
