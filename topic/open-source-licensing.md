---
title: Open-Source Intellectual Property Governance
---
A governance framework for keeping organizational-memory infrastructure open, self-hostable, portable, and resistant to proprietary capture while protecting the software commons and supporting sustainable commercial activity. Rizom’s runtime, platform, and core repositories may use AGPL-3.0, while the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces may use Apache-2.0 to enable broad reuse and commercial extensions without surrendering stewardship of shared infrastructure. Portable Markdown data, complete export and re-import, documented ownership deeds, public repositories, contributor terms, security review, and a Developer Certificate of Origin should provide verifiable data sovereignty and clean intellectual-property records.

Rizom B.V. may initially hold copyrights and trademarks, with core intellectual property and the Rizom brand eventually transferred to an independent foundation or stichting governed as an open commons. The commercial BV should retain a perpetual licence-back, service and partner registries, client relationships, certified practices, and related revenue, separating commons stewardship from commercial operations without undermining the business. Investors consequently receive exposure to services and ecosystem activity rather than ownership of the core commons.

Copyright and software licences should be complemented by clear trademark and brand governance. The Rizom name, logo, product names, certification marks, domains, package names, and namespaces should be subject to clearance searches, published usage rules, registry controls, and approval requirements. Forks and modified versions should use distinct names and avoid misleading association. Ongoing administration should cover assignments, renewals, genuine-use compliance, opposition monitoring, coexistence arrangements, fallback branding, and potential international registration, preserving client trust, portability, and freedom from vendor lock-in.
