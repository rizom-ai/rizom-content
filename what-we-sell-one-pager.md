# AI didn't break your knowledge system. It exposed that you never had one.

*Rizom makes an organisation's memory visible, then makes it work.*

Every organisation runs on knowledge that lives in people rather than in systems: who knows what, whose judgment settles which questions, how information actually moves. It is rarely written down, because it always worked. It stops working when a key person leaves, a team grows fast, or an AI tool is asked to do a job nobody ever specified.

**What we sell.** Five products. Each stands on its own, and each one's result is the next one's starting point.

- **See it — Team Workshop · €1,500 half-day in-house (up to 15 people), €750 online (90 minutes).** For a team that has just added AI and wants to work out how a team with an AI member actually runs. Together, the team draws who does what, who decides what, and what the AI is for. You leave with a shared picture of the memory the team runs on. If the picture shows gaps, the audit is the next step.
- **Audit it — Knowledge Audit · €5,000 fixed, three weeks.** An independent finding on where your organisation depends on individuals. Interviews with 6–10 people (what does this person know that nobody else does, and what breaks when they leave?), a half-day mapping workshop, and an audit report: where knowledge lives, whose judgment counts, where it leaks, and three to five interventions ranked by cost and impact. The report is yours; there is no next step you are obliged to take. Credits in full toward a build.
- **Build it — Build · €15,000 fixed as launch client (standard €35,000).** The audit's map becomes working memory: a Brain, shared memory for a team of people and AI, capturing from where work already happens — the chat where a decision lands, the email thread, the conversation — so it stays current without anyone maintaining a knowledge base, deployed on your infrastructure or hosted by us, in daily use by one team. The difference between the two prices is earned, not timed: a launch client co-delivers, stands as the public reference case, and carries the first-mover risk.
- **Run it — Retainer · €4,000/month as launch client (standard €7,000).** Hosting, upgrades, and support — we run the Brain so your team just uses it. A Living Memory Score tracks how well the memory is actually working: continuous and behavioral, derived from the work itself. Monthly, cancellable.
- **Spread it — Partner track.** For organisations that want to open a whole field rather than fix one team: co-develop the first Brain application for their vertical and become the certified partner who delivers it there (see [[innovation-lead-pilot-offer-outline]]).

**Who this is for.** Whoever owns how the work flows: a director, a head of operations, a programme lead. Organisations whose knowledge *is* the work — especially fields where knowledge leaves faster than it accumulates: young professional fields, high fluctuation, fast-moving practice. Current pilots run in innovation networks, sustainability, and art & academia.

**No lock-in at any step.** We sell the work, not the software. The platform is open source (AGPL) and self-hostable, and every piece of content is plain, portable markdown. Memory this important belongs to the organisation that creates it: the workshop and the audit leave you with a document, the build leaves you with a system you can run yourself, and the retainer is cancellable because the exported system can always be self-hosted. You stay because it works, not because you are stuck.

**The foundation underneath.** The approach is built on transactive memory systems — four decades of organisational psychology on why some teams consistently outperform equally talented ones, extended by Rizom to teams that now include AI. The science, and why it matters now, is one more page: see the companion pager "Living Memory."
