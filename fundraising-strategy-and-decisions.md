# Fundraising Strategy & Decisions

Course set 2026-08-03 (working session, Jan Hein). Internal — restricted.

## The course (settled)
Rizom is **foundation-led / non-dilutive-first**, not a VC platform play. The stichting owns the IP + "Rizom" mark (AGPL commons); the **BV stays deliberately valuable** = certified practice + partner registry + client relationships/services revenue + a **perpetual licence-back**. Frame it as *structure* (foundation-and-BV), not *identity* (foundation-vs-VC) — that is what stops the decision boomeranging. Guardrail: never transfer IP to the stichting without the licence-back, or the BV is hollow.

## Founder / company reality (the clock)
- Pre-revenue, alpha (shipping toward beta/RC); **EUR 0 in the BV**.
- One founder needs income. Personal draw **EUR 3,500/mo** (a floor, tight).
- Rizom opex **EUR 1,700/mo** = EUR 800 fixed (growing with trial brains) + EUR 900 amortised NL travel (6-week stints, ~2/yr, ~EUR 5,500 each).
- **Monthly nut = EUR 5,200.**
- Personal runway ~2 months (really ~1.3 once opex is counted) → **first paid close must land within ~8 weeks.**
- **The single binding constraint is closing** — the founder's self-identified weak spot, on a new proposition. The business is not the risk; closing cadence is.

## Closing — two tracks
- **Track A:** put a closer on the 15% acquisition share (higher-leverage than any dev hire).
- **Track B (now):** founder runs scripted, warm-only, small-paid-first-step closes (the close kit).
- The paid-pilot close is the survival lever; grants/investors are too slow for the cliff.

## Pricing & unit economics (locked)
- **Knowledge Session EUR 5,000** — the small paid first step (Survey → Workshop → Playbook); credits toward a pilot.
- **Pilot build EUR 15k early-adopter / EUR 35k standard.**
- **Retainer EUR 4k/mo early-adopter / EUR 7k/mo standard.**
- Split **60/25/15** (delivery / acquisition / commons). No stichting yet → founder keeps ~100% now (75% steady-state after the 25% commons remit).
- Near-term: one EUR 5k Knowledge Session ≈ covers a month. ~1 session/mo, or 1 retainer + a top-up, = break-even.

## The raise
- **Bridge ask ≈ EUR 45k** — cover ~6 months of ramp + buffer + seed the closer hire. Not a growth round; a bridge to services self-sustainability.
- What BV equity buys: the practice + registry + client relationships + licence-back — NOT the IP. Pitch honestly (convertible / revenue-based / SAFE), mission-aligned; do not use platform-multiple math over foundation-owned IP.
- **Loans/convertibles:** founder is open, but they are a *later* instrument. **UNIIQ** (InnovationQuarter, Zuid-Holland, ~EUR 300k convertible) is the natural growth vehicle once pilots become retainers and the model is proven. Not now (too big, too slow, re-opens the equity question at 6x scale).

## Non-dilutive (parallel)
- **NLnet / NGI Zero** — EUR 50k open-source grant, best fit (AGPL + AT Protocol), reopens ~Sept 2026. Proposal drafted; needs founder bio + repo links. Apply day-one.
- **WBSO** — needs Dutch payroll; founder has an NL address and can run payroll (chose to skip the accountant residency-check). A subsidy, not fast cash.
- **AWS Activate (~USD 5k) + FUTO microgrant** — fast, small, on-brand.
- **Sovereign Tech Agency** — bigger ticket, later, as adoption grows.
- Grants do not rescue the 8-week cliff — the paid pilot close does.
