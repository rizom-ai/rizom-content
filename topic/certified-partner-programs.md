---
title: Certified Partner Programs
---
The Rizom Certified Partner program defines a durable model for expanding delivery capacity through external practitioners. It launches with a single Certified Practitioner track using capability tags for facilitation, domain expertise, implementation, and deployment. Entry is based on apprenticeship through a real paid engagement rather than an exam or fixed curriculum. Certification is maintained through continuous standing in a public registry, with progression from candidate to active to steward. The model separates certification from ownership, allowing partners to participate at the Collective tier without automatically receiving Core status. This creates a repeatable framework for trust, capability recognition, and network growth.
