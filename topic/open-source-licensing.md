---
title: Open Source Licensing
---
Rizom’s open-source licensing framework uses AGPL-3.0 for the core runtime and brains repository, while the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces use Apache-2.0 to enable broad reuse and commercial extensions. These licensing details, repository metadata, README references, and contribution terms should be established before accepting external contributions. A Developer Certificate of Origin will clarify contributor rights and account for organizational succession by Rizom B.V. and its successors, supporting clean intellectual-property management.

Licensing operates alongside copyright and trademark governance. Rizom B.V. will initially hold copyrights and trademarks, with a planned transfer to an independent Rizom Foundation or stichting. Open software may be referenced, modified, and extended with required attribution, but the Rizom name, logo, product names, wordmark, certification marks, domains, package names, and namespaces remain subject to published policies, registry controls, or approval. Forks and modified versions must use distinct names and avoid misleading association. This framework preserves the software commons, clarifies ownership and contribution rights, and protects brand integrity while enabling partners, service providers, and commercial ecosystem participants to build on Rizom under clear legal terms.
