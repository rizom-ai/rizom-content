# TMS Services Plan — Knowledge Session → Pilot

Internal. Companion note: rizom-product-plan. Pricing is NOT set here — it follows fundraising-strategy-and-decisions (locked 2026-08-03). This note describes what actually gets delivered at each priced step, and the rule that protects product work from services work. Drafted 2026-08-04.

## The gap this closes

TMS reaches a buyer two ways today: a workshop, or a bespoke engagement (Weave). The Knowledge Session is priced at EUR 5,000 as Survey to Workshop to Playbook, but the Survey and Playbook halves have no defined method — only the workshop is designed. That asymmetry is the gap.

In rizom-consulting-one-pager-en the whole body of work still appears as one bullet, "Knowledge workshops — One session, one map", with no number attached.

## What each step delivers

**Workshop** — designed and ready. Half-day in-house up to 15 people, or 90 minutes online. Full outlines exist in the yeehaa brain as workshop-design-hybrid-human-ai-teams and full-outline-online-workshop-designing-hybrid-human-ai-teams-90-min. The room feels the fragility; nobody leaves with a plan.

**Survey** — needs building. 6 to 10 individual interviews before the workshop, running the Fragility Test one-on-one: what does this person know that nobody else does, and what breaks if they leave tomorrow. This is what turns a good session into a diagnosis.

**Playbook** — needs building. The canvas filled in as-is rather than aspirational, across the three TMS dimensions: Expertise Map for Specialization, Trust Boundaries for Credibility, Operating Playbook for Coordination. Plus 3 to 5 interventions ranked by cost and impact.

## Buyer

Not engineering. COO, CTO, Head of Operations, or a programme director inside an institution. Triggers worth listening for: a key person resigning, a reorg or merger, rapid hiring, or an AI rollout that underdelivered.

That last one is the wedge. The reframe already written into the workshop — AI does not break your knowledge system, it exposes that you never had one — lets this be bought on an AI budget while fixing an organisational problem.

## Why this is the survival lever

fundraising-strategy-and-decisions is explicit: one Knowledge Session at EUR 5,000 roughly covers a month, and the first paid close must land within about eight weeks. The Knowledge Session is the smallest thing a warm contact can say yes to. Everything in this note exists to make that yes easy to deliver against.

## What to build

Interview protocol for the Survey, mostly derivable from the workshop exercises. Playbook template. One page on rizom.ai/work with the EUR 5,000 visible. Ask Weave for a testimonial — it converts a precedent into a case study.

## The cap

Services revenue always feels more urgent than product work because it is closer and more certain. Left unmanaged this track eats every quarter and the retainer tier in rizom-product-plan never gets built out.

Rule: cap this track deliberately. It funds the year; it is not the year. When a Knowledge Session competes for the same week as a pilot milestone or a grant deadline, the pilot or grant wins — they are the only work that stops scaling with hours.

A session will sometimes surface a Brain opportunity. Take it when it happens. It is a bonus path, not a pipeline: the Datacampus and Port of Rotterdam conversations did not originate this way.
