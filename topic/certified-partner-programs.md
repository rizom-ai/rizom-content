---
title: Certified Partner Programs
---
The Rizom Certified Partner program is a foundation-governed framework for expanding delivery capacity through trusted external practitioners. It launches with a single Certified Practitioner track, using capability tags for facilitation, domain expertise, implementation, and deployment rather than fixed sub-tiers. Entry is earned through apprenticeship on a real paid engagement, not an exam or standardized curriculum. Certification is distinct from ownership: partners may participate at the Collective tier without receiving Core status. The foundation governs certification, public listings, registry standing, and use of Rizom Certified marks. Practitioners progress from candidate to active to steward, maintaining certification through continuous standing in the registry. The commercial practice participates as the first listed partner under the same rules. Shared pricing bands and a role-based 60/25/15 allocation for delivery, client sourcing, and commons stewardship provide a repeatable economic model for trust, capability recognition, and sustainable network growth.
