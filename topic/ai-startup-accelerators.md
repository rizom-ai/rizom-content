---
title: AI Startup and Open-Source Funding Programs
---
The content evaluates two distinct funding pathways for AI ventures and projects. AI Grant’s accelerator, aigrant.com, targets pre-seed and seed-stage AI-native startups with an equity-based package reportedly including an uncapped SAFE, substantial cloud and AI platform credits, a summit, and a demo day. It may suit Rizom’s technical profile and help offset infrastructure costs, but participation is a strategic fundraising commitment involving dilution, accelerator obligations, and time-limited application batches. Separately, AI Grant’s open-source program, aigrant.org, offers potentially non-dilutive support of roughly $5,000–$50,000 in cash or computing resources for projects such as machine-learning infrastructure, data formats, bindings, training research, and scientific datasets. Its eligibility rules, application process, and deadlines are unclear, making access discretionary. Rizom’s AGPL-licensed open-source AI platform could justify outreach, although its application-layer focus may be less aligned than core ML infrastructure. The two programs should be assessed independently despite their similar branding.
