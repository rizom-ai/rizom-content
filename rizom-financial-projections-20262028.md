# Rizom Financial Projections 2026–2028

Derived from [[rizom-operating-model]] (canonical split and policies) and [[investor-one-pager]] (pricing). Drafted 2026-08-06. All amounts EUR thousands unless stated. These are driver-based scenarios, not forecasts: every number traces to an assumption below.

## Assumptions

**Pricing** (from the one-pager): Knowledge Session 5; early-adopter build 15, retainer 4/mo; standard build 35, retainer 7/mo. New engagements price at standard from mid-2027.

**Split mechanics** (from the operating model): every engagement divides 60 delivery / 15 acquisition / 25 commons. The B.V. books: the 25% commons on all system revenue; the 15% acquisition share on engagements it sources; the 60% delivery share only where founder/employees deliver. As collective members take over delivery, their 60% leaves the B.V. — modelled as a rising co-delivery share (2026: 0%, 2027: 30%, 2028: 50% in base).

**Partner economics** (from the one-pager): a modestly active partner generates ~238/yr system revenue → ~60/yr commons flow to the B.V., plus ~10 one-time certification fee. Unproven until the first partner certifies — treated as the model's main risk.

**Hosting**: 300/brain/mo average, 30% COGS.

**Costs**: founder draw 60/yr (66 in 2028 conservative, 72–80 base/growth). Hire #1 platform-ops 90/yr fully loaded net of WBSO; hire #2 onboarding/DX 95/yr; hires follow the operating-model gate (commons flow covers the prior hire, or funding closes the gap). Tools/ops/travel 15–40/yr scaling with activity.

## Results (B.V. revenue → net)

**2026 H2** — Conservative: 27 rev, −7 net. Base: 55 rev, +21 net. Growth: 79 rev, +29 net.

**2027** — Conservative: 161 rev (engagements 132, hosting 29), costs 135, **+26**. Base: 329 rev (engagements 217, partner flow 40, hosting 72), costs 206, **+123**. Growth: 628 rev (engagements 346, partners 120, hosting 162), costs 319, **+309**.

**2028** — Conservative: 361 rev, costs 239, **+122**. Base: 740 rev (engagements 304, partners 260, hosting 176 margin-relevant), costs 393, **+347**. Growth: ~1,330 rev (partners 650, hosting 540, engagements ~300), costs ~640, **+690**.

## Thresholds (the gates, in numbers)

- **Hire #1 self-carried**: commons flow ≥ 90/yr → ≥ ~360/yr system revenue (own practice plus ~1.5 modest partners). Base crosses during 2027.
- **Hire #2 self-carried**: commons flow ≥ 185/yr → ≥ ~740/yr system revenue. Base crosses during 2028; growth crosses mid-2027 only with funding.
- **Ghost line** (hosting margin alone carries the infra team): ~73 brains for two FTE. Base reaches it during 2028.

## What this says about the raise

No scenario needs money to survive: the hire-gating policy keeps even conservative cash-positive from 2027. The raise is therefore genuinely growth capital — it buys the difference between base and growth, i.e. the flywheel roughly 18 months earlier. Sized from the drivers: **EUR 250–300k**, used as: hire #1 pull-forward (~45), hire #2 pull-forward (~90), partner certification program (~50), reference deployments and cohort operations (~40), commercial capacity (~50), buffer (~25). This replaces the withdrawn EUR 75k bridge framing: not runway, acceleration.

## Sensitivities (the three that matter)

1. **Retainer churn** — each lost standard retainer removes 84/yr system revenue (21 commons). Two losses turn base-2027 net from +123 to ~+60.
2. **Pilot slip** — one quarter's slip in the three pilots pushes 2026 H2 negative in all scenarios; harmless if hires stay gated, expensive if hired ahead of it.
3. **Partner ramp** — all 2028 upside concentrates here and it is unproven. Test (unchanged from the one-pager): a first certified partner within twelve months. If it fails, 2028 base degrades toward conservative (~+120) — sustainable, not compounding.

## Honesty notes

- The model is high-leverage by construction: partner commons flow and hosting margin carry almost no marginal cost. That is the model's promise and its untested claim — stated here once so the investor brief doesn't have to hedge every line.
- INC/node-zero costs are inside "reference deployments" and capped per the operating model (one brain unfunded); the cohort is grant-funded or doesn't happen at this scale.
- WBSO is assumed netted into hire costs; if not claimed, add ~25/yr per development hire.
