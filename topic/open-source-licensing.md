---
title: Open Source Licensing
---
The licensing strategy uses AGPL-3.0 for the core Rizom runtime to deter proprietary hosted forks and reduce the risk of closed commercial capture, while keeping the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces under Apache licensing so partners can build and commercialize extensions. A Developer Certificate of Origin is planned for future contributions to clarify contributor rights and support clean intellectual-property succession. Copyright and trademark assets initially remain with the BV, with formal transfer to a future foundation planned. Trademark controls distinguish open code from controlled brand use: descriptive references are permitted, while the Rizom wordmark and Certified marks require compliance with a published usage policy and, for certified marks, approval as a listed partner; forks must use distinct names. This structure preserves the software commons while enabling professional services, implementation, and broader commercial participation.
