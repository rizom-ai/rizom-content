# Knowledge Session for Schools

**Understand where critical knowledge lives, what depends on individual people, and where practical improvements are worth making.**

## A practical three-week diagnostic

Schools rely on more than documents and systems. Important knowledge often lives with experienced teachers, administrators and leaders: how exceptions are handled, where information can really be found, who understands a recurring problem, and whose judgment colleagues rely on.

The Knowledge Session makes that invisible knowledge system easier to see. It gives the school a practical playbook of risks and opportunities before any decision about technology is made.

It begins with an exploratory conversation, at no cost and no obligation, to decide together whether a session is useful.

## What happens

1. Five to eight individual staff conversations of about 45 minutes each.
2. A half-day workshop to map knowledge, recurring questions, information flows, decision authority and important dependencies.
3. Synthesis into a school-owned playbook with practical recommendations.

## What you receive

1. A current-state map of where important organisational knowledge sits and where the school is vulnerable to knowledge loss or bottlenecks.
2. Three to five interventions ranked by expected value and effort. These can include process, documentation, role or technology changes.
3. A recommended first use case for a digital knowledge solution, only if the evidence supports one.
4. A baseline and success criteria that make any later pilot measurable.
5. A playbook you keep and can act on with or without KA21 or Rizom.

## What we ask from the school

1. Five to eight staff members for individual interviews.
2. Relevant staff for a half-day workshop, and a suitable room.
3. A school sponsor who can help clarify priorities and make decisions.
4. Three weeks from the first interview to the final playbook.

## Designed to be low risk

No pupil data, IT integration or system access is required. The session is about organisational knowledge and resilience, not staff performance. Individual comments are not attributed unnecessarily. The school controls its information and keeps the resulting playbook.

## Fixed price: €5,000

The Knowledge Session stands on its own. Commissioning it creates no obligation to continue into a technology deployment.

## What can happen afterwards

If the session identifies a valuable and feasible opportunity, a bounded first deployment can follow, separately agreed, to test one use case with real users. Only if that proves worthwhile would anything broader be considered. Every step requires a new decision.
