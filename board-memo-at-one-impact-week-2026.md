# Attending AT ONE Impact Week 2026

Board memo · Rizom B.V. · Jan Hein Hoogstad · 26 August 2026 · Decision requested before 1 September

> **Decision requested:** approve five founder-days and an estimated €1,500–2,500 plus a
> to-be-confirmed delegate contribution for Jan Hein to attend AT ONE Impact Week
> (8–12 September, Suntec Singapore) as co-host of the Planetir–Kaphera–House of Wonder
> delegation, with the commercial and partnership plan below.

## 1. Context

Rizom's Southeast Asia fractional Chief AI Officer campaign is live: seven outreach threads
opened this week (Monk's Hill, Alpha JWC, Openspace, SG Growth Capital/EDBI, SC Ventures,
plus two intro chains), six warm Singapore contacts identified and ready for meetings, and
 the one-pager pack complete. Separately, Daphne Laan (Planetir) has invited Jan Hein to
co-host their delegation at Impact Week; the public event page already lists him as a host.
The trip decision and the Planetir partnership question are related but distinct; this memo
treats them separately (sections 3 and 4).

## 2. What the week is

AT ONE Impact Week is a five-day "well-being economy" forum hosted by TPC and NO.17
Foundation. The delegation runs an exhibition (a live ecosystem map with AI-assisted
matchmaking), two interactive labs (sovereign data & stewardship; regenerative capital),
and a curated evening sailing event (30–50 guests; themes: regenerative capital, ecosystem
collaboration, sovereign data; the Dutch Ambassador to Singapore is an invited guest,
unconfirmed).

## 3. The commercial case

The trip converts an outreach campaign into meetings, at the exact moment the campaign
needs it:

- **Six warm contacts in Singapore** (embassy startup liaison, founder-mentors,
  fund-adjacent professionals) get "I'm in town for Impact Week, 8–12 September" instead
  of "call sometime" — a materially stronger ask, batched into one week without reading as
  a sales tour.
- **Any fund or corporate thread that responds in the next two weeks** (Monk's Hill, Alpha
  JWC, Openspace, SG Growth Capital, SC Ventures) converts to an in-person meeting — the
  single best conversion event a fractional pitch has.
- **Hosting gives the visit a legitimate, non-commercial answer** to "what brings you
  here," which is the posture the whole campaign is built on: substance first, pitch
  downstream.
- Practical add-ons: the cross-border tax/structuring conversation (KPMG contact) and
  engagement-contract groundwork (funds lawyer contact) happen in person the same week.

## 4. The Planetir partnership question (a separate decision)

Hidden inside the trip decision is a second one: do we want Planetir as a deployment
partner? The overlap is unusually strong — their language ("sovereign data," "knowledge
held in trust, not extracted," "AI that distributes rather than concentrates
intelligence") is Rizom's thesis, and their announced "experimental open data space" for
 the Impact Week ecosystem has, as far as their materials show, *no named infrastructure
behind it*. Self-hosted brains are the natural candidate, and Kaphera's steward-ownership
model makes them an aligned rather than extractive counterpart.

**Proposed position:** this trip scopes the partnership; it does not start it. Concretely:

- Before the week: one conversation with Daphne and Mehemed asking what the open data
  space runs on, and whether they want a Rizom-backed pilot.
- Any build work happens only under written pilot terms (paid pilot or formal partnership
  with named scope). No free infrastructure delivered on goodwill during or after the
  event week.
- If the pilot conversation goes nowhere, the co-host role stands on its own and nothing is
  lost.

## 5. The case against, at full strength

- **Five founder-days off the product**, two weeks after opening an active outreach
  campaign that may itself demand responses.
- **Cash cost** including a delegate contribution whose size is currently unknown.
- **Register risk:** the delegation sits in the "human consciousness" track, and "what if
  love became infrastructure" sits far from the fractional-CAIO positioning on the same
  public LinkedIn week. The two voices must be managed deliberately.
- **Absorption risk:** delegation programming can consume all five days; ceremonial
  commitments expand to fill available founders.
- **Partnership drift risk:** "collaboration" with mission-driven partners has a known
  failure mode — unpaid build work justified by alignment.

## 6. Mitigations

- Two to three half-days ring-fenced for the commercial track before the delegation
  schedule fills; warm-contact meetings booked this week, before departure.
- The Planetir infrastructure conversation held before the trip, with the pilot-terms rule
  above.
- The delegate contribution confirmed with Daphne before committing; if it exceeds reason,
  renegotiate the host role or withdraw with notice.
- The outreach pack (one-pagers, follow-up messages) is already send-ready, so campaign
  responses cost minutes, not days, during the week.

## 7. What success looks like

Agreed now, reviewed within two weeks after the trip — against these numbers, not against
mood:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Warm-contact meetings held (of six) | ≥ 5 |
| Fund / corporate threads converted to in-person meetings | ≥ 2 |
| Planetir infrastructure follow-up with written scope or explicit no | 1 |
| Sprint-qualified leads within 30 days of the week | ≥ 1 |

## 9. Recommendation

Go — conditional on (a) the delegate contribution being confirmed and reasonable, and
(b) the ring-fenced commercial half-days being protected in the delegation schedule before
departure. The trip converts an already-running campaign into meetings, scopes a genuinely
aligned partnership under discipline, and the host role costs little beyond presence while
answering for the visit. The honest alternative — staying home — saves roughly €2,000 and
five days, and leaves seven threads to convert over email against competitors who show up.
