# AI Grant — Research and Fit Assessment

Researched 2026-07-29. Resolves the checklist item in [[certified-partner-program-action-checklist]]. Context: an aigrant.org bookmark in the docs brain was being advertised by a derived agent skill as if Rizom ran a grant program; this note documents what the programs actually are and whether Rizom should pursue them.

## Two distinct programs, one brand

**aigrant.org — open-source grants (non-dilutive)**
- $5,000–$50,000, "no strings attached", paid as cash or as compute (compute via andromeda.ai, the Friedman/Gross GPU cluster).
- Founded 2017 by Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross.
- Track record skews toward ML infrastructure and research: llama-cpp-python bindings (abetlen), the GGUF file format (philpax), fast-training research (TySam), audio/music datasets, medical imaging, physics simulation.
- **No public application process.** The site has no form, no deadlines, no eligibility text — grants appear discretionary. Only contact surface: support@aigrant.org (listed on the accelerator site).

**aigrant.com — the accelerator (equity)**
- $250,000 on an uncapped SAFE, plus ~$600k in credits ($350k Azure + $250k across AI platforms including Anthropic and OpenAI), summit + demo day.
- Run day-to-day by Hersh Desai and Lenny Bogdonoff; Nat and Daniel contributed $10M.
- For pre-seed/seed AI-native product startups; "no credentials required"; global; solo founders accepted.
- Four batches funded to date; **Batch 4 applications are closed**. No Batch 5 announcement found as of 2026-07-29.

## Fit for Rizom

**The open-source grant (aigrant.org): worth one email, not a project.**
The brains platform is a real open-source AI project and the AGPL move strengthens the story, but historical grantees are mostly ML plumbing (formats, bindings, training) rather than application-layer knowledge tools, and there is no application to fill in. Realistic play: a short, concrete email to support@aigrant.org after the relicense lands — repo link, one paragraph on what the platform is, one on what $25–50k of compute/cash would fund (e.g. embedding/model costs for pilot fleets). Cost of trying: an hour. Expected value: low but non-zero.

**The accelerator (aigrant.com): the more serious fit — and a strategic decision, not a form.**
Rizom B.V. is literally the target profile: seed-stage, AI-native product, technical founder. The package (~$850k value, including Anthropic/OpenAI credits that directly offset brain-running costs) lines up with the 2026 Q2 fundraising goal in the Mother Doc. But it is equity (uncapped SAFE) and an accelerator commitment — a fundraising-strategy decision for the core owners, not an opportunistic application. If pursued: watch for Batch 5 opening; the application window has historically been short (days-to-weeks between announcement and deadline, announced via Nat Friedman's X account).
