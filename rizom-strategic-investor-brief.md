## What Rizom is

Every organisation loses knowledge — the context behind decisions, the reasoning of a project, the expertise that leaves with people — and the tools that promise to fix it are closed platforms where the knowledge becomes the vendor's asset. Rizom Brains is the opposite: an open, self-hostable brain an organisation fully owns — plain markdown on their own infrastructure, AGPL-licensed so nobody can enclose it.

We sell the work, not the software. Brains are deployed and run for organisations by a curated collective, compensated per engagement — the team scales with the work, not with payroll. The platform is open; the practice, the brand and the network are the business.

## The model

Every engagement splits 60% delivery / 15% acquisition / 25% commons; the commons share funds the platform, its small employed infrastructure team, and the foundation that stewards it. Three revenue lines switch on in sequence: the direct practice (the proof), a certified partner network (commons flow with no delivery cost), and hosted brains (the Ghost model: a foundation owns the open-source IP, the operating company's hosted service funds the whole).

The entity structure is deliberate: an independent foundation will own the core IP and brand (founding in progress), with the B.V. operating under an exclusive commercial licence — perpetual, irrevocable, surviving change of control. Contributor ownership accrues through a STAK as it is earned. Investors hold B.V. shares directly; the cap table stays clean.

## The capital plan

Two rounds, staged by what they buy rather than by need — with hiring gated on cash flow, even the conservative scenario is cash-positive from 2027, so no round is survival money.

**Now:** friends & family, ~EUR 75k, converting into the institutional round.

**On evidence, indicatively 2027 H1:** institutional/strategic, EUR 250–300k. It opens when the base case is visibly tracking: pilots converted to recurring retainers, the first certified partner underway, reference deployments public, hosted revenue visible. It buys the growth scenario roughly eighteen months early — the second platform hire, the partner certification program, reference deployments, commercial capacity.

## Why strategic capital

The model's value concentrates in network effects: partner certification, sector reference deployments, a growing hosted fleet. An aligned strategic investor accelerates all three; purely financial capital cannot. What we want today is the relationship — know the model, watch the evidence arrive, be positioned to move when the round opens. What we offer today is transparency: the operating model, the projections, and the thresholds below are written down and shareable.

## The tests we hold ourselves to

- The share of pilots that choose managed hosting.
- Commons flow crossing the hire gates (~EUR 360k, then ~EUR 740k system revenue per year).
