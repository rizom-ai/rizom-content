---
title: Open-Source and Self-Hosted Software Governance
---
A governance and product framework for keeping organizational-memory infrastructure open, self-hostable, portable, and resistant to proprietary capture while enabling sustainable commercial activity. Rizom’s core runtime, platform, and repositories use AGPL-3.0-only, with data stored in portable Markdown and supported by complete export and re-import, enabling organizations to deploy and operate the platform on their own infrastructure or migrate away from managed hosting without vendor lock-in. The AT Protocol, OAuth-enabled MCP server, plugin SDK, deployment tooling, themes, and related interfaces support interoperability, self-installation, and partner extensibility; surrounding SDK and integration packages may use permissive Apache-2.0 licensing to encourage commercial reuse without restricting the shared core.

Commercial value comes from managed hosting, deployment, training, operational support, certification, and expert services rather than from restricting access to the runtime or paywalling self-hosting. A reusable operator playbook makes self-hosting a scalable product and go-to-market model. Data ownership, public repositories, security review, documented contribution and intellectual-property practices, and Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off provide verifiable sovereignty and clean provenance.

Platform intellectual property and the Rizom brand are intended to be stewarded by an independent Dutch stichting established as an open commons, with a statutory stewardship purpose and independent board representation. The commercial company retains an exclusive, durable licence to operate the business while continuing to manage services, hosted operations, partners, certifications, and client relationships. This separates commons governance from commercial activity and aligns investment with services and ecosystem growth rather than ownership of core infrastructure. Trademark clearance, usage rules, registry controls, and distinct naming for forks protect the brand while preserving the freedom to modify, deploy, and extend the platform.
