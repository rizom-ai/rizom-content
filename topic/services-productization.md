---
title: Services Productization
---
The topic covers converting bespoke consulting and implementation work into a clearly packaged, repeatable, and increasingly scalable commercial offering. This includes structured knowledge sessions with defined diagnostic, learning, and playbook stages; bounded pilot builds; and recurring retainers with consistent pricing, scope, delivery methods, and outcomes. Productization requires resolving commercial details such as hosting, upgrades, support, seats, usage rights, and procurement requirements so buyers can understand the offer and providers can deliver it consistently. The model may guide customers from a free self-hosted product through paid services and into recurring engagements, while distinguishing scalable software from labor-dependent talent or consulting work. Because services can generate near-term revenue but consume capacity needed for longer-term product development, deliberate limits and clear boundaries are necessary to protect scalable product work.
