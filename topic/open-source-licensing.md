---
title: Open Source Licensing
---
The program establishes a legal architecture that combines strong copyleft protection for the core runtime with permissive licensing for extension surfaces. The runtime moves to AGPL-3.0 to prevent proprietary hosted forks, while the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces remain Apache-licensed so partners can freely build and sell extensions. A Developer Certificate of Origin is planned for future contributions. The approach treats the codebase as a commons while preserving commercial opportunities around implementation, practice, and network participation. Trademark controls complement the licensing strategy by reserving certified marks for approved partners and requiring renamed forks.
