---
title: Open-Source and Self-Hosted Software Governance
---
A governance and operating framework for keeping organizational-memory infrastructure open, self-hostable, portable, and resistant to proprietary capture while enabling sustainable commercial activity. Rizom’s core runtime and platform use AGPL-3.0-only, with knowledge stored in portable Markdown and supported by complete export and re-import. Organizations retain ownership of their data and can deploy the platform independently, migrate away from managed hosting, or use managed hosting as an optional convenience without vendor lock-in.

Interoperability through the AT Protocol, OAuth-enabled MCP server, plugin SDK, deployment tooling, themes, and related interfaces supports self-installation and partner extensibility. Selected SDK and integration packages may use Apache-2.0 to encourage commercial reuse. Commercial value comes from hosting, deployment, training, operational support, certification, partner services, and expert implementation rather than restricting access to the runtime or self-hosting.

Governance separates the open commons from commercial operations. An independent Dutch stichting is intended to steward the platform’s core intellectual property and brand under an open-commons purpose with independent board representation, while Rizom B.V. operates hosted services, client engagements, and partner activities under an exclusive, durable licence. Public repositories, transparent contribution and intellectual-property practices, security review, and Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off support accountability. Trademark and registry controls protect the brand without limiting the freedom to modify, deploy, extend, and migrate the platform.
