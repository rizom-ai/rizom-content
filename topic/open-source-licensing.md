---
title: Open-Source and Self-Hosted Software Governance
---
A governance and operating framework for keeping organizational-memory infrastructure open, self-hostable, portable, and resistant to vendor lock-in while enabling sustainable commercial activity. The core platform is released under AGPL-3.0-only, with knowledge stored in portable Markdown and supported by complete export and re-import. Deploying organizations retain ownership and control of their data, can run the platform independently, migrate away from managed hosting, or use hosting as an optional operational convenience.

Interoperability through the AT Protocol, OAuth-enabled MCP server, plugin SDK, deployment tooling, themes, and related interfaces supports self-installation and partner extensibility. Selected SDK and integration packages may use Apache-2.0 to encourage commercial reuse, allowing certified partners to build and commercially exploit extensions. Commercial value comes from hosting, deployment, upgrades, training, operational support, certification, partner services, and expert implementation rather than restricting access to the runtime or self-hosting.

Governance separates the open commons from commercial operations. An independent Dutch stichting is intended to steward the platform’s core intellectual property and brand under an open-commons purpose with independent board representation, while Rizom B.V. operates hosted services, client engagements, and partner activities under an exclusive, durable licence. Public repositories, transparent contribution and intellectual-property practices, security review, and Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off support accountability. Trademark and registry controls protect the brand without limiting the freedom to modify, deploy, extend, or migrate the platform.
