---
title: Open Source Grants
---
The content examines aigrant.org as a source of non-dilutive funding for open-source projects. Grants reportedly range from $5,000 to $50,000 and may be provided as cash or computing resources. The program has historically supported machine-learning infrastructure, data formats, bindings, training research, and scientific datasets. It does not publish a conventional application process, eligibility criteria, or deadlines, making access largely discretionary. Rizom’s open-source AI platform may have a credible basis for outreach, especially after its AGPL relicensing. However, the expected opportunity is limited because the platform is primarily an application-layer knowledge tool rather than core ML infrastructure.
