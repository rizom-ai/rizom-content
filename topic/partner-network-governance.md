---
title: Partner Network Governance
---
The foundation, established as a long-term legal steward such as a stichting, governs partner certification, registry ownership, and use of certified marks. A public, machine-readable registry records partner status, stewards, delivered engagements, and compatibility to support ongoing trust and accountability. Certification does not confer Core ownership, and the commercial practice, while co-delivering engagements and serving as the first listed partner or vouching for participants, remains subject to the same rules as external partners. Published price bands and a transparent economic model allocate revenue according to delivery, client origination, risk, and commons stewardship, with a defined share supporting platform development and foundation operations. Together, these mechanisms distinguish participation from ownership and create accountable, transparent coordination across a distributed service ecosystem.
