---
title: Ecosystem-Based Organizational Design
---
Ecosystem-based organizational design is a regenerative model for building resilient organizations, communities, and professional networks through distributed ownership, shared knowledge, coordinated action, and autonomy without rigid hierarchy. It enables diverse participants to contribute skills and resources, develop relationships and capabilities, and create value collectively through transparent governance, facilitation, and removal of organizational blockages. Durable implementations may separate stewardship of intellectual property and other core assets from commercial development and delivery—for example, through an independent foundation or stichting alongside a commercial entity. Clear arrangements for trademarks, copyrights, governance, dispute resolution, and board independence support continuity and institutional resilience, while open-source infrastructure and partner networks extend the ecosystem’s capacity to deliver services. Participation can range from broad communities to project collectives and ownership-oriented cores, allowing the organization to adapt, regenerate value, and grow from multiple aligned points.
