---
title: Open Source Licensing
---
Rizom’s licensing strategy uses AGPL-3.0 for the core runtime and repository to preserve software freedom and discourage proprietary hosted forks, while the plugin SDK, AT Protocol contracts, and theme interfaces use Apache-2.0 to support broad reuse and commercial extensions. Before accepting external contributions, repositories should include accurate license fields, README references, and contribution terms. A Developer Certificate of Origin will clarify contributor rights, account for succession by Rizom B.V. and its successors, and support clean intellectual-property management.

Licensing is coordinated with trademark governance and future intellectual-property transfers. Rizom B.V. will initially hold copyrights and trademarks, with a planned transfer to an independent Rizom Foundation. Open software may be referenced and extended with required attribution, but use of the Rizom name, logo, product names, wordmark, certification marks, domains, package names, and namespaces remains subject to published policies, registry controls, or approval. Forks and modified versions must use distinct names and avoid misleading association. This framework protects the software commons and brand integrity while enabling partners, professional-service providers, and commercial ecosystem participants to build on Rizom under clear legal terms.
