---
title: Ecosystem-Based Organizational Design
---
Ecosystem-based organizational design is a regenerative approach to building resilient communities, organizations, and professional networks through distributed ownership, shared knowledge, and effective coordination without rigid hierarchy. It creates conditions for diverse participants to connect, contribute skills and resources, develop personally, and act collectively while retaining their autonomy and identities. Key practices include curating meaningful relationships, moderating shared spaces, facilitating development, and removing organizational blockages. Participation may range from a broad community to project-based collectives and ownership-oriented cores, with transparent, distributive decision-making, compensation, and responsibility. The model enables organizations to adapt, regenerate value, and grow from multiple points through aligned interests and collective capacity.
