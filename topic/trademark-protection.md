---
title: Trademark Protection
---
A practical strategy for protecting the RIZOM word mark through an EU trade mark application, supporting an international partner ecosystem. Before filing, conduct clearance searches across TMview, EUIPO, BOIP, company registers, and broader web sources, with particular attention to potentially conflicting marks such as Rizom-Lab and RIZOMUV, especially in software-related classes. EUIPO is preferable to a Benelux-only registration because of the brand’s international scope. The proposed application covers specific, genuinely used goods and services in Nice classes 9, 35, 41, and 42, including software, business consultancy, education, workshops, SaaS, and software development. Risk management may include narrowing software specifications, seeking a coexistence agreement, and retaining less-contested consulting and training services. The strategy should also address opposition exposure and deadlines, genuine-use requirements, assignment to a future stichting, recording changes, renewal obligations, and international expansion through the Madrid Protocol.
