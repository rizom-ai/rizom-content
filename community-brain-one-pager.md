# Community Brain — One-Pager

*For hubs, workspaces, and campuses — the offer behind Track 2 of [[sales-pipeline-knowledge-sessions-brain-for-space]]. Sales-facing companion to [[what-we-sell-one-pager]]; same engagement ladder, hub vertical. Canonical text of the 1-page PDF (rizom-onepager-community-brain.pdf).*

# Your product is your community. Its memory shouldn't be one person deep.

A hub sells what happens between members: the introductions, the expertise, what last month's event sparked. Today that memory lives in the community manager's head and a chat archive nobody reads — and it walks out the door with every departure. A Community Brain is living memory the hub owns: open source, on your own infrastructure, exportable at any time. We deploy and run it; you own it.

## What it does

**Events that don't evaporate.** Every session, talk, and hallway thread captured as working memory the next event builds on — capture, not archive.

**Members who find each other.** Expertise findable, who-knows-what visible, introductions that suggest themselves.

**Onboarding by memory.** New members land in the accumulated context instead of a welcome email. The community manager is relieved, not replaced.

**Yours, not a platform's.** Plain portable files on your infrastructure, AGPL open source, export to self-hosting whenever you want. That is the trust the offer stands on.

## How it starts

- **Knowledge Session — EUR 5,000.** Survey, workshop, playbook, run with your community team. A few weeks, fixed price, credits toward the build.
- **Build & retainer.** Pilot build from EUR 15k (early-adopter), then a monthly retainer from EUR 4k that keeps the memory alive.
- **The hub deal.** Part of the retainer can be settled in resident desks at fair value, invoiced both directions. And a hub is more than a client: member deals the brain generates earn the hub the 15% acquisition share of the Rizom split — the first hubs enter the partner economics.

## Why hubs (internal note, not on the PDF)

A hub brain is a showroom: the members watching it work are founders and companies — Rizom's client profile — and the hub shows it off as its own differentiator. The stream's economics are client + venue + channel in one deal. Barter settles space, never replaces invoicing; a barter client is still a client on the books.
