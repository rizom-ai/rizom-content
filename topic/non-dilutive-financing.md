---
title: Mission-Aligned Financing Strategy
---
Rizom’s financing strategy combines earned services revenue, non-dilutive funding, and selectively deployed growth capital to extend runway, protect ownership of core intellectual property, and build a self-sustaining open-source commons. In the near term, paid services provide the most immediate cash flow: EUR 5,000 Knowledge Sessions can lead to EUR 15,000 early-adopter or EUR 35,000 standard pilots, followed by EUR 4,000 or EUR 7,000 monthly retainers. Sessions may be credited toward pilots, while successful pilots should convert into recurring retainers that fund delivery, customer acquisition, and commons development. Grants, subsidies, R&D wage support, and technology or cloud credits—including NLnet/NGI Zero, WBSO, AWS Activate, FUTO, and the Sovereign Tech Agency—can fund open-platform infrastructure without equity dilution. Larger instruments such as UNIIQ, bridge financing, revenue-based financing, or a proposed EUR 250,000–300,000 raise should complement rather than replace earned revenue and grants. External capital is best treated as growth capital, accelerating hiring, partner certification, reference deployments, commercial capacity, and cohort operations rather than covering structural losses. Conservative, base, and growth scenarios should test whether funding can advance the growth trajectory by approximately 18 months, with hiring gated by recurring commons revenue and disciplined thresholds. Key sensitivities include retainer churn, pilot delays, and the uncertain pace of partner certification. Separating ecosystem infrastructure funding from commercial operations preserves capital efficiency and supports transparent, mission-aligned deployment of capital without relying on high-growth venture financing.
