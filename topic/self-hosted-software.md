---
title: Self-Hosted Software
---
Rizom’s self-hosted runtime is free and released under AGPL-3.0, positioning open-source software as both a product option and a credibility-driven entry point for institutional buyers. It serves organisations unable to use SaaS because of procurement, security, sovereignty, or operational requirements—not merely those that prefer self-hosting. The surrounding technical assets, including the OAuth-enabled MCP server, plugin SDK and system, atproto contracts, theme interfaces, and deployment tooling, support genuine self-installation and partner extensibility. Apache-licensed interfaces allow partners to build and sell extensions, while the Rizom mark and associated commons remit remain distinct. Commercial value therefore comes from expertise, deployment, training, supported operation, and potentially trained operators rather than restricted access to the runtime. An operator playbook is important for reducing dependence on one-person delivery and making self-hosting a scalable product architecture and go-to-market model rather than simply a free tier.
