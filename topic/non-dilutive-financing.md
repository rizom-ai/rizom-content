---
title: Mission-Aligned Financing Strategy
---
Rizom’s financing strategy combines non-dilutive funding with services-based operating revenue to extend runway, protect ownership of core intellectual property, and build toward self-sustainability. Non-dilutive sources—including open-source grants, subsidies, R&D wage support, and technology or cloud credits such as NLnet/NGI Zero, WBSO, AWS Activate, FUTO, and the Sovereign Tech Agency—can fund open-platform and commons infrastructure without equity dilution. In the near term, however, paid services are the most immediate source of cash flow: a EUR 5,000 Knowledge Session leads into EUR 15,000 early-adopter or EUR 35,000 standard pilots, followed by EUR 4,000 or EUR 7,000 monthly retainers. Sessions may be credited toward pilots, and pilots should convert into recurring retainers that support delivery, customer acquisition, and the open-source commons. Grants and larger instruments such as UNIIQ complement rather than replace bridge, revenue-based, or services financing, since they may arrive too slowly for immediate runway needs. Separating funding for ecosystem infrastructure from commercial operations preserves capital efficiency and avoids relying on high-growth venture financing where mission-aligned support and earned revenue are more appropriate.
