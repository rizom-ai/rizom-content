---
title: Non-Dilutive Financing
---
Rizom prioritizes non-dilutive funding—including open-source grants, subsidies, R&D wage support, and technology or cloud credits—to develop its open platform and commons infrastructure without transferring ownership of core intellectual property. Potential sources include NLnet/NGI Zero, WBSO, AWS Activate, FUTO, and the Sovereign Tech Agency, with larger instruments such as UNIIQ considered after pilots generate recurring retainers. These funds complement, rather than replace, bridge financing: grants often arrive too slowly to address immediate runway needs, while bridge or revenue-based capital supports pilot delivery, customer acquisition, and the transition to a self-sustaining services practice. Keeping ecosystem infrastructure and commercial operations funded through distinct instruments extends runway, preserves capital efficiency, and avoids using high-growth venture financing where mission-aligned support is more appropriate.
